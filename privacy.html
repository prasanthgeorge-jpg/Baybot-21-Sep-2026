Privacy Policy

How we handle your information

Effective date: October 22, 2026
Business: Bay Bot Dynamics LLC doing business as BayBot Dynamics

BayBot Dynamics respects your privacy. This notice explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal information when you visit our website, request information, or communicate with us about autonomous robotics products. It does not cover data collected by deployed robotics products, which will be addressed separately where applicable.

Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Your name, business email address, telephone number, company, and job title.
  • Information you provide through inquiries, demonstration requests, forms, and correspondence.
  • Communication records, including call dates, duration, follow-up requests, and consent or opt-out preferences.
  • Website technical information, such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited, where collected by our website or service providers.
  • Call recordings, transcripts, and AI: We may collect call recordings and transcripts and use AI-powered tools to conduct conversations, summarize calls, and identify follow-up actions. We obtain consent for automated or AI-generated voice calls where required before making those calls. Our recording and transcription practices are described under Calls and communication preferences below.
  • Public and third-party sources: We may obtain business contact information, including names, job titles, business email addresses, and telephone numbers, from company websites, professional directories, referrals, and business contact providers. We use this information to identify relevant business contacts and assess interest in our robotics products. Public availability of contact information does not itself establish consent to marketing calls.

How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and arrange demonstrations or consultations.
  • Explain our robotics products, capabilities, and business applications.
  • Provide requested educational materials and promotional communications, subject to applicable consent requirements.
  • Maintain customer relationships, communication preferences, and suppression lists.
  • Operate and secure our services, prevent misuse, and meet legal obligations.

Calls and communication preferences

Providing a telephone number does not, by itself, constitute consent to every type of marketing communication.

Where required, we obtain consent before making promotional calls or using automated dialing, prerecorded messages, or AI-generated voices. You may ask us to stop marketing calls during any call or by contacting us below. We maintain an internal do-not-call list to honor these requests.

Call recording and transcription: BayBot Dynamics may record and transcribe calls to document customer inquiries, prepare follow-up communications, train staff, and improve service quality. We use communications service providers, including Twilio, and recording, transcription, or AI service providers as applicable to process these communications on our behalf. Before recording or transcription begins, we inform all participants and obtain their consent. If anyone declines, we do not record or transcribe the call. Access to recordings and transcripts is restricted to authorized personnel and service providers who need access for these purposes. We retain these records only as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this notice and to meet applicable legal obligations, then delete them.

When we share information

We may disclose information to service providers supporting our operations, such as communications providers, including Twilio, email providers, hosting providers, and customer relationship management services, as needed to provide their services.

We may also disclose information where legally required or necessary to protect rights, safety, or security.

We do not sell personal information or share telephone numbers or communication consent records with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

How we protect information

We take reasonable steps to limit access to personal information and protect it against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Retention

We retain personal information only as reasonably necessary for the purposes described here, including legal obligations and recordkeeping. We may retain limited consent and opt-out records to document permissions and prevent unwanted future contact.

Your choices and rights

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information, or to withdraw communication consent. Depending on your location and applicable law, additional rights may apply. We may need to verify your identity before responding, and some information may be retained where permitted or required by law.

Cookies and third-party websites

Cookies and similar technologies: Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to support its operation, security, and functionality. You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking certain cookies may affect how the website functions.

Our website may link to third-party websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own notices.

Updates

We may update this notice as our practices change. We will post the revised notice with an updated effective date and provide additional notice where required.

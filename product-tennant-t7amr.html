Overview

About the Tennant T7AMR

The T7AMR autonomous floor scrubber delivers autonomous floor cleaning with intelligent navigation and reliable performance. Help reduce labor demands, improve operational efficiency, and conserve water and detergent with available ec-H2O NanoClean® technology. Powered by BrainOS®, an advanced artificial intelligence and robotic technology platform, the T7AMR is specifically designed for autonomous cleaning in commercial spaces, working safely alongside employees and customers.

Key Features

What Sets the Tennant T7AMR Apart

BrainOS® Commercial Autonomy

Autonomous navigation handles dynamic real-world environments, automatically routing around pedestrians, luggage, and displays.

High-Efficiency Scrub Paths

26-inch (650 mm) scrub path handles medium to large facilities with up to 45,760 sq ft/hr coverage.

ec-H2O NanoClean® Cleaning

Cleans without harsh chemicals, reducing detergent handling and leaving floors slip-resistant and clean.

Hygenic® Easy-Clean Tanks

Fully accessible solution and recovery tanks simplify daily rinse-out and prevent mold, bacteria, and odor buildup.

Extended Lithium-Ion Runtime

High-capacity lithium battery option delivers up to 6.5 hours of continuous scrubbing per charge.

Reporting & Fleet Management

Weekly performance reports and cellular usage metrics verify completed routes and daily cleaning coverage.

Overview

About the Tennant X16 SWEEP

The X16 SWEEP Autonomous Floor Sweeper is designed to deliver increased efficiency in complex industrial environments. Engineered for tough manufacturing and warehouse facilities, it sweeps bulk debris, sand, pallet chips, and fine dust autonomously with multi-stage filtration and a hydraulic high-dump hopper.

Key Features

What Sets the Tennant X16 SWEEP Apart

Hydraulic High-Dump Hopper

5.3 cu ft (150 L) hopper hydraulically lifts and dumps debris directly into standard industrial dumpsters.

Wide 46-Inch Sweeping Path

Robust main broom and dual side brushes clear wide aisles in single passes at up to 49,750 sq ft/hr.

Multi-Stage Dust Filtration

Advanced filtration system captures fine airborne particulate to maintain clean facility breathing zones.

Complex Aisle Autonomy

3D perception sensors allow safe autonomous navigation around pallet stacks, forklifts, and staging areas.

Rugged Industrial Build

Steel frame and impact-resistant body panels withstand demanding 24/7 industrial production environments.

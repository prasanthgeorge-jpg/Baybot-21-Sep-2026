BayBot ZW193

Portable Intelligent Bathing System: A Full Bath in Bed, With One Caregiver

The BayBot ZW193 brings a complete, comfortable bath to the bedside. It sprays warm water and recovers the wastewater at the same moment, so a bedridden or less mobile person can be washed from head to toe without leaving the bed and without wetting the bedding.

Request a Demo
Overview

About the BayBot ZW193

Bathing is one of the hardest daily tasks in care. Moving a person to a bathroom takes two or three people, carries a real risk of falls and strain, and leaves many residents and patients going without a proper wash for days. The ZW193 removes the transfer. One caregiver wheels it to the bed, fills the built-in tank, and gives a thorough bath that keeps the person in a safe, private and familiar place.

It is compact, quiet and portable, so it works equally well in an aged care room, a hospital ward, a rehabilitation centre or a family home.

Deployed In

  • Aged care and nursing homes
  • Hospitals
  • Rehabilitation centers
  • Home care
  • Mobile bathing services
  • Hospice and palliative care
Key Features

What Sets the BayBot ZW193 Apart

Wash and suction at the same time

Water is sprayed and drawn back in real time by a high-speed brushless turbine. Bedding and mattress stay dry, with no dripping and no puddles, and the person gets a true full-body bath, not a quick sponge wipe.

One caregiver, no transfer

Traditional bathing often needs two or three people to move someone to a bathroom. The ZW193 is operated by a single caregiver at the bedside, which cuts physical strain and removes the risk of a fall during transfer.

Dignity and privacy

Only the part of the body being washed is uncovered. The person stays in their own bed and room, which is calmer and far less exposing than a bathroom transfer.

Constant, comfortable water temperature

Water heats on demand with no long wait, and temperature is adjustable from 35 to 45 °C and held steady, so the bath stays warm and safe.

Built-in clean and waste tanks

A 3.5 L clean-water tank and a 2.85 L wastewater tank are built in, so there are no buckets or external containers. Clean and waste water run on separate circuits to prevent backflow and cross-contamination, and the screen warns you about low water or a full tank.

Gentle, shaped cleaning heads

A U-shaped hydrophilic sponge head follows the contours of the body. A separate silicone head is used for washing hair, so one machine covers body and hair.

Spoken step-by-step guidance

Real-time voice prompts and status messages guide caregivers and family members through each step, so new users can bathe someone confidently.

Quiet, safe operation

A brushless motor keeps operating noise at or below 65 dB. A leakage-protection plug cuts power if a fault is detected, and automatic tip-over protection shuts the unit down if it is tilted past 35° or knocked over.

How It Works

Core Functions

Function 1

Bed Bathing Without Transfers

Wheel the unit to the bedside, fill the clean tank, set the temperature and start. The person is washed where they lie, which keeps them comfortable and lets care teams bathe people who could not safely be moved.

Function 2

Intelligent Suction That Adapts

Variable-frequency suction reaches up to -10 kPa and adjusts automatically to the water flow. It handles sponge bathing, shower bathing and hair washing, and leaves linen dry.

Function 3

Built-In Monitoring and Protection

The system keeps watch over tank levels, tilt and operating status. It raises on-screen alerts when you need to refill or empty a tank, and protects the user and caregiver with automatic shutdown if something is wrong.

Function 4

Easy to Carry and Store

A telescopic carry handle, hose management and a dedicated shower-handle holder keep everything together, so one person can move the unit between rooms, vehicles and storage.

Specifications

Technical Details

Water System

  • Clean water tank: 3.5 L
  • Wastewater tank: 2.85 L
  • Water temperature: adjustable, 35 to 45 °C
  • Heating: instant heating, no preheat wait
  • Runtime per tank: up to 13 minutes in sponge bath mode, up to 10 minutes in shower mode

Performance and Safety

  • Suction: adjustable up to -10 kPa, variable-frequency control
  • Motor: brushless turbine and motor
  • Operating noise: 65 dB or less
  • Tip-over protection: automatic shutdown beyond 35°
  • Electrical protection: leakage-protection plug
  • Cleaning heads: sponge head (body) and silicone head (hair)

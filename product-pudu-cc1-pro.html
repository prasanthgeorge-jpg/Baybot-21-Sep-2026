PUDU CC1 Pro

Autonomous Multi-Function Floor Cleaning Robot for Commercial Facilities

The PUDU CC1 Pro is a professional-grade autonomous cleaning robot designed for demanding commercial environments. It integrates sweeping, scrubbing, vacuuming, and mopping into a single intelligent system, delivering consistent, high-quality floor cleaning.

Overview

About the PUDU CC1 Pro

AI-powered 4-in-1 commercial cleaning robot (sweeps, scrubs, vacuums, mops) with real-time rear camera stain detection and automatic spot re-cleaning. 1500-3000㎡/h coverage with up to 9h runtime (silent mopping); 15L clean/15L waste tanks with optional auto-refill docking station

Key Features

What Sets the PUDU CC1 Pro Apart

AI Spot Scrubbing

AI vision detects common wet stains such as coffee spills, sauces and puddles in real time and plans cleaning routes to them, at a cleaning rate of 1,500–3,000 m²/h.

Real-Time Cleaning Performance Detection

A rear AI camera checks the floor behind the robot, detects stains left behind and triggers automatic spot re-cleaning. It also produces a heatmap after each task.

AI Cleaning Intensity Control

Boosts power for stubborn stains and switches to eco mode on clean areas. Floor type detection sweeps and vacuums on hard floors and vacuums only on carpet.

AI Components Self-Monitoring

The rear AI system monitors the cleaning components, spots maintenance issues that could cause secondary contamination, and triggers marking, touch-up or self-cleaning.

4-in-1 Versatile Cleaning

Sweeping, scrubbing, vacuuming and dust mopping in one robot, as an all-in-one cleaning solution.

PUDU VSLAM+ Positioning

Visual SLAM and LiDAR fusion navigate complex spaces with no QR codes needed. With AI spot cleaning the robot covers 5,000–8,000 m².

Omni-Sense Safety

AI-driven perception constantly detects static and moving obstacles, using an obstacle-recognition model that keeps expanding.

IEC 63327 Certification

PUDU states that the upgraded cleaning system complies with IEC 63327, with enhanced durability, easier maintenance and improved cleaning power.

How It Works

Core Functions

Function 1

Automatically performs sweeping

Automatically performs sweeping, scrubbing, vacuuming, and mopping in a single cleaning cycle.

Function 2

Turn Cleaning Data into Insight

A waste hotspot map shows where the most waste collects, so you can schedule cleaning. A cleaning performance heatmap marks stains left after several cycles, and an operation dashboard tracks task completion, maintenance frequency and equipment alerts.

Function 3

Connects to Your Building Systems

No system opening is needed, and the robot connects modularly to existing processes. Manage it with the PUDU Link app, with optional e-gate and elevator control.

Function 4

Work Station Series

With the optional docking station the robot returns to charge on its own, refills and drains water automatically, and self-cleans its roller brush and squeegee.

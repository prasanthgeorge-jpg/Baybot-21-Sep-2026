Overview

About the Tennant X6 ROVR

Powered by SelfPath™ AI and the XC1 Charging Dock, the X6 ROVR™ delivers maximum autonomous cleaning coverage across diverse environments. Intelligent navigation, adaptive route optimization, and automated charging help reduce operator involvement and free teams for higher-value tasks. The X6 ROVR is designed for expansive retail, healthcare, airport, and educational campuses.

Key Features

What Sets the Tennant X6 ROVR Apart

SelfPath™ Adaptive AI Navigation

Dynamically calculates the most efficient cleaning paths and continuously adapts to changing facility layouts.

Automated XC1 Charging Station

Hands-free auto-docking recharges lithium-ion batteries and enables continuous scheduled cleaning cycles.

Impressive 6-Hour Runtime

Extended run duration easily completes full facility cleaning passes on a single charge.

Expansive 26-Inch Scrub Width

Cleans up to 75,000 sq ft per solution tank with powerful dual disk scrubbing action.

Heavy Down Pressure Control

Variable down pressure up to 120 lbs (54 kg) deep cleans tough scuffs and ingrained soil.
